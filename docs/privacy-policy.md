# Privacy Policy: CAIIB Study

Last updated: 16 November 2026

CAIIB Study ("the app") is an independent study app for the CAIIB examinations. It is published by
**[PUBLISHER NAME]** ("we"). Contact: **[SUPPORT EMAIL]**.

## The short version

In this version the app does not collect, transmit or share your personal data. Everything you do in the app
stays on your device.

## What the app stores on your device

- Your study progress: which lessons you completed, your answers to practice questions, your self-ratings,
  review schedule, bookmarks, and the results of timed mocks.
- Your settings: selected paper, preferred session length and theme.

This information is kept in the app's private storage on your phone. We cannot see it. Clearing the app's
storage or uninstalling the app deletes it permanently; we cannot restore it.

## What we do not collect

- No account, name, email address, phone number or password is needed or requested.
- No location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or device identifiers are accessed.
- No advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services are used, and no advertising identifier is read.
- No payment information is handled. This version has no purchases.

## Network use

The app works offline. The study content is installed with the app. The app does not send your progress or any
personal data to our servers or to third parties.

## Children

The app is intended for adults preparing for a professional banking examination. It is not directed at children
under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

## Security

Your data stays in the app's private storage, protected by your device's operating system. We recommend using a
device lock.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version adds accounts, synchronisation, purchases or any data collection, we will update this policy
and the Google Play Data safety form before that version is released, and describe the change in the app.

## Your choices

You can delete all data held by the app at any time by clearing its storage in Android settings or by
uninstalling it.
