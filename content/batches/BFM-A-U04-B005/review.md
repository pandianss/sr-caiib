## Where the courseware needs care

- It lists **DAT** among the eleven Incoterms and omits **DAP** (printed pp. 116-117), while saying DPU replaced DAT. The items teach the 2020 list (DAP in, DAT out).
- Its four-category grouping (departure, main carriage paid, main carriage unpaid, arrival) is the earlier edition's; the items use the two families of 2020.
- It teaches **ISBP 745**, the edition the syllabus names. The ICC's current edition is ISBP 821 (2023). The card says so; no item tests ISBP 821.
- Its case studies use figures that were **not** reused. The insurance case in the items uses new figures (USD 41,600 invoice and USD 46,500 cover) because the courseware's case numbers would have been copied.
