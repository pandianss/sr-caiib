## Differences found between the courseware and the Regulations as amended

- Exposure ceiling: the courseware states 5 per cent and 10 per cent of the parent's Tier 1 capital; the Regulations replaced this on 6 July 2021 with norms the IFSCA specifies. Not tested as current.
- Retail deposit reserve ratio of 3 per cent: the Regulations leave it to the IFSCA; stated as the courseware's figure only.
- Licensing authority: courseware says Reserve Bank permission; the Regulations name the IFSCA.
- IFSC Banking Company (subsidiary, USD 50 million) and the general permission for activities appear only in the amended Regulations.

## Limits of the evidence

- Amendments after July 2023 were not searched for. The IFSCA's directions on specified currencies, reserve ratios and exposure were not retrieved.
- GIFT City dates, numbers of companies and FPI tax relaxations are the courseware's statements.
- The reviewer is the authoring agent; no independent or human review.
