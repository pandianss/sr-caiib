Seventeen items. Written 14 November 2026 by Claude Sonnet 5.5.

## Evidence

Courseware S03 printed pp. 135-139 was read directly (printed page n = PDF page n + 17). Write-off, set-off, EEFC, Diamond Dollar and EDF waiver rules were checked against RBI's Master Direction on Export of Goods and Services
(`RBI_MD_EXPORT`, sections C.23, C.26, A.4 to A.6 and C.1; saved and fingerprinted; same source and applicability note as batch BFM-A-U05-B001). The export finance concepts (pre-shipment and post-shipment) come from the courseware and are definitional.

## Where the courseware differs from the Master Direction

- Diamond Dollar Account: the courseware says at least two years' track record; the Master Direction says three. The Master Direction is taught.
- The courseware's netting of export receivables for SEZ units and its EDF exemption list were not compared and are not taught.

## Deliberately left out

The quantitative export credit rules on printed pp. 138-140 (periods of 180 and 360 days, the FOB deduction, the MCLR linkage of 1 April 2016, running account, sub-supplier credit): RBI's export credit directions were consolidated in 2025 and no current text was retrieved, and the MCLR linkage is likely out of date.
The FEDAI rule on the effective date of realisation was not retrieved.
