## Limits of the evidence

- The Guarantees Regulations do not mention standby letters of credit. That a standby works like a guarantee comes from the courseware.
- The courseware's rules for commercial standby credits for imports (selected importers and supplier credit reports) rest on older circulars that the 2026 Regulations replace; they were not verified and are not taught. The two held items about them (batch BFM-A-U04-B004, D006 and D016) are withdrawn.
- ISP 98 and UCP 600 themselves were not retrieved (ICC publications).
- The date on which the Regulations took effect was taken from the notification (publication in the Official Gazette); a Master Direction page seen in search results says 10 January 2026. The Gazette date was not checked.
- The reviewer is the authoring agent; no independent or human review.
