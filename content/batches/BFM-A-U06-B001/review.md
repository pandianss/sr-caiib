## Where the courseware differs from the current Regulations

- **Maturity.** The courseware has 3, 5, 7 or 10 years by end-use; the Regulations have a three-year MAMP with a one-to-three-year option for manufacturers up to USD 150 million.
- **Cost.** The courseware has an all-in-cost cap of benchmark plus 500 bps (450 for rupee ECB); the Regulations have market-based "cost of borrowing", with the trade credit ceiling applying to ECBs under three years.
- **Limit.** The courseware has USD 750 million a year under the automatic route; the Regulations have the higher of USD 1 billion outstanding ECB or 300% of net worth.
- **End-use and ratios.** The courseware's list and its 7:1 liability-equity ratio are replaced by Regulation 3A.
- **Trade credit spread.** The Regulations state 250 bps "or as prescribed by the Reserve Bank"; the courseware says 300 bps. The spread is not tested.

If the examination follows the courseware, the examiner may use the older figures; the items teach, and card D001 explains, the position at the cut-off.
