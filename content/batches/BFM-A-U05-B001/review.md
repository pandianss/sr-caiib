## Where the courseware differs from the Master Direction

- The courseware lists ACU settlement without the Sri Lanka relaxation (outside ACU from 8 July 2022). The Master Direction's relaxation is taught.
- The courseware's three-month re-import undertaking for refunds is not in the Master Direction; its own conditions are taught.
- The courseware's INR trade settlement text (printed pp. 131-132) is not taught: the corresponding paragraph was substituted on 17 July 2026 and the version at the cut-off was not separately verified.
- The courseware's list of exemptions from declaring exports is not itemised: the Master Direction refers to Regulation 4 without listing cases.
