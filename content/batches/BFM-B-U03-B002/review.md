## Differences found between the courseware and the Directions

- Equity: the courseware says 9 per cent specific and 9 per cent general (18 per cent); the Directions say 11.25 per cent specific (or rating-based, if higher) plus 9 per cent general.
- Rating agencies: the Directions' table also lists Brickwork; the courseware's list has six agencies.
- Trading book and classification: the courseware's HFT/AFS wording predates the HTM/AFS/FVTPL classification (see batch BFM-B-U02-B001).
- The courseware's gross income exclusion refers to the "held to maturity" category; the Directions say banking book securities.

## Limits of the evidence

- The Directions copy is updated to 7 October 2026, after the cut-off; some inserted text takes effect from 1 October 2026 and amendments after 28 November 2025 were not compared. Only the standard cases are taught.
- Housing loan risk weights are taught from the table for loans sanctioned on or after 7 June 2017; later sanction windows were not read.
- Basel Committee texts were not read.
- The reviewer is the authoring agent; no independent or human review.
