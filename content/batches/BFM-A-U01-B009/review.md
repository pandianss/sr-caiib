# Batch BFM-A-U01-B009 — evidence and review record

Paper BFM, unit "Exchange Rates and Forex Business" (`BFM::MOD_A::U01`), objectives LO_01 (hours of business) and LO_07 (FEDAI rules).
Twenty-five items. Written 14 November 2026 by Claude Sonnet 5.5.

## Evidence

FEDAI's own AR Circular No. 02/2024 of 5 April 2024 (`FEDAI_RULES_10E`), which annexes the full text of the FEDAI Rules, 10th Edition, updated up to 5 April 2024, was downloaded, saved locally and fingerprinted in `content/sources/registry.json`.
The tracked amendments (23 January 2020, 2 November 2020, 8 February 2022 and 5 April 2024) are shown in the text, and the circular's table of revisions shows what changed in 2024. No later edition or amendment was found; a 2026 FEDAI newsletter exists but announces no new edition.
The courseware (S03 printed pp. 28-32) corresponds to the earlier wording (for example the 4.30 p.m. customer cut-off and an older benchmark list in Rule 8); the items teach the rules as updated.

## What this batch resolves

- The earlier batch BFM-A-U01-B007 (Rules 1 and 6 restated from the courseware) stays unpublished as superseded; its Rule 1 wording was outdated.
- The held crystallisation items in BFM-A-U04-B003 and the Normal Transit Period in BFM-A-U05-B003 depended on these rules. Crystallisation of export and import bills, the Normal Transit Period table and the effective date of realisation are now taught from the source.
