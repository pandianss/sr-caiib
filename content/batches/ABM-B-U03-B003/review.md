# Review record: ABM-B-U03-B003

Paper ABM, Module B unit 3 (courseware unit 12), gap closure: sexual harassment at work, the PoSH Act 2013 and the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita offences against women (objective LO_07, registered in this batch). Seventeen items: 3 concept cards and 14 choice questions. Record written 14 November 2026.

## Evidence

- `WCD_POSH_HANDBOOK_2015`: the Ministry of Women and Child Development handbook of November 2015 (definition of sexual harassment, three-month written complaint, Local Committee for enterprises with fewer than 10 workers, false complaints).
- `PIB_POSH_2026`: the PIB release of 6 February 2026 (coverage of all women, definitions of aggrieved woman and employee, Supreme Court directions on committees).
- `NCRB_BNS_IPC_TABLE`: the NCRB corresponding section table (BNS 74 to 79 against IPC 354, 354A, 354B, 354C, 354D and 509; the note that section 106(2) was not to come into force on 1 July 2024).
All three are saved locally with hashes and the tests check key phrases against them.

## Limits

- The Act and the Sanhita texts (India Code) were unreachable, so these are government summaries and a concordance, not statutes. Penalties, ingredients of the offences, the composition of the Internal Committee and later amendments of the Act were not checked and are not taught.
- The 10-worker boundary is described differently in the handbook ("fewer than 10") and the PIB release of Supreme Court directions ("more than 10"); the items avoid the exact-10 case.
- The BNS commencement date of 1 July 2024 is inferred from the table note and marked `needs_review`.
- The Hindu Succession Act statement and the 2016 amendment note of the courseware remain untaught (ledger gap kept).

## Drafts and originality

Tables and procedures are written afresh; section numbers and definitions are factual. Answer positions are spread across A to D by construction. Distractor explanations are brief.
