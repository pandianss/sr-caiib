## Where the courseware needs care

- The courseware's extra advance-remittance conditions (import licence, direct payment to the supplier, six months or three years for physical import and fifteen days for evidence; printed p. 154) are only partly in the general part of the Master Direction (the six months, three years and fifteen days appear in the aviation section); they are not taught here.
- The extension test is the point most easily confused: for imports the limit is the lower of USD 1 million and 10% of two years' average remittances; for exports (batch B001) it is the higher of USD 1 million and 10% of three years' realisations. Both are taught and contrasted.
