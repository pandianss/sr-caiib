## Differences found between the courseware annexure and the Directions

- CET1: the Directions include the AFS reserve and require audited or limited-reviewed statements for quarterly profit recognition; the courseware states an older condition.
- Add-on factors for market-related contracts: the Directions (as substituted from 10 March 2026) give 0.25/0.50/1.50 for interest rate and 1/5/7.5 for exchange rate contracts; the courseware table is older. The Directions are taught.
- Rating agencies: seven domestic agencies (courseware lists six with SMERA, now Acuite); unrated claims are 150 per cent above ₹200 crore aggregate banking-system exposure.
- Haircut table: the courseware gives 13 for securitisation rated A to BBB over five years; the Directions give 24. The Directions are taught.
- CCFs, the 55 and 25 per cent discounts, the collateral list, E* formula, 8 per cent currency haircut, holding periods and maturity adjustment agree with the Directions.
