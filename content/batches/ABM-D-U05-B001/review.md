# Review record: ABM-D-U05-B001

Paper ABM, Module D unit 5 (courseware unit 30, Compliance Culture and GRC Framework): compliance culture, its benefits and warning signs, the seven elements of a compliance programme, regulators' attributes of a culture of compliance, RBI's minimum supervisory expectations, compliance organisation and resources, the GRC framework and its benefits, whistle-blower policy, process and components, whistle-blowing law in India as the courseware presents it, and the reasons for compliance failures (objectives LO_01 to LO_06, registered in this batch). Seventy items: 11 concept cards and 59 choice questions. There are no quantitative items. Record written 14 November 2026.

## Evidence

Courseware `S01` printed pp. 634 to 655 (PDF pp. 649 to 670), read as page images. The only primary check is `RBI_CCO_2020` (RBI circular of 11 September 2020), which confirms that the compliance policy sets expectations on tone from the top, accountability, incentive structure and effective communication and challenge; those phrases are tested against the saved text.

## Limits and what was held back

- The unit is almost entirely the courseware's account and commentary. The ten regulator attributes, the benefits and signs of culture, the model whistle-blower policy and the reasons for compliance failures are not RBI requirements.
- The whistle-blower legal framework (Companies Act 2013 sections 177(9), 206 to 229, 208, 210, 211; the Rs 50 crore borrowing threshold; the old SEBI clause 49; the Whistle Blowers Protection Act 2011 "yet to come into force"; the 2004 PIDPI Resolution and its 2013 amendment) was not checked against primary text. Clause 49 has been replaced by the SEBI Listing Regulations 2015, and the Act's status at 30 June 2026 was not verified from the Gazette; searches found no notification bringing it into force. The items test the PIDPI account and policy features as the courseware's account only; no item tests the Companies Act section numbers, the Rs 50 crore threshold or the Act's status.
- Not taught: the list of RBI penalties on banks (amounts, banks, dates, section 46(4)(i) and 51(1) citations), the global fine estimates and the Rs 19.41 crore aggregate figure.
- The August 1992 Ghosh Committee origin of the compliance officer role and the April 2005 Basel Committee paper are as the courseware states them (needs_review).
- An unclear model policy item on written permission of the chief executive for public dealing concerns is not taught.

## Drafts and originality

Tables and procedures are written afresh. Answer positions are spread across A to D by construction. Distractor explanations are brief.
