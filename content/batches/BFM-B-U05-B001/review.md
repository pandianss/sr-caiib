## Difference found between the courseware and the Directions

The courseware describes the 2022 credit derivatives framework (market-maker net owned funds of ₹500 crore, single-name CDS only, a limited list of reference obligations). The Credit Derivatives Directions, 2026, in force from 25 June 2026, supersede it. The items teach the 2026 framework at the level of scope, participants and reporting; the 2022 thresholds are not tested.
