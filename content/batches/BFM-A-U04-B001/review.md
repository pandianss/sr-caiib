## Where the courseware needs care

- It describes the **revocable** credit as a type (printed p. 92), although UCP 600 removed it and the courseware itself says all UCP 600 credits are irrevocable. The items teach the irrevocable position and note the 2007 removal.
- The export and import flow descriptions pass through RBI's EDPMS and IDPMS (printed pp. 95-98); these systems belong to the exporter and importer facilities unit and are not taught here.
- The courseware's example of ISBP is **ISBP 745**; the ICC's current guide is ISBP 821. The ISBP batch will say so.
