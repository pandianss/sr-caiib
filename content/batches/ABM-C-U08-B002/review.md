# Review record: ABM-C-U08-B002

Paper ABM, Module C unit 8 (courseware unit 24, Restructuring/Rehabilitation and Recovery), part 2: SMA categories and CRILC, the resolution framework (review period, inter-creditor agreement, resolution plan, independent credit evaluation, additional provisioning), prudential norms after restructuring, change in ownership, projects under implementation (DCCO), and sale and transfer of stressed loans (objectives LO_05 to LO_08, registered in ABM-C-U08-B001). Sixty-two items: 9 concept cards, 4 worked examples, 39 choice questions and 10 numerical questions. Record written 14 November 2026.

## Evidence

Courseware `S01` printed pp. 518 to 527 (PDF pp. 533 to 542), read as page images. The courseware reproduces the 2019 Prudential Framework, the 2022 master circular and earlier circulars. The facts are taught from the primary Directions: `RBI_RSA_2025` (Resolution of Stressed Assets Directions, 2025, paragraphs 6 and 9 to 124) and `RBI_TDCR_2025` (Transfer and Distribution of Credit Risk Directions, 2025, paragraphs 55 to 92 and 145 to 146). The tests check key phrases against the local copies.

## Limits and what was held back

- Not taught because the 2025 Directions do not carry them or the windows have expired: the 2016 MSME revival framework (Rs 25 crore committee), the GST transition relaxations, the one-time restructuring windows of 2019 to 2021 (the COVID-19 frameworks and the Kamath Committee ratios), the courseware's older DCCO deferment table, its project finance standard asset provisioning table, and the 2014 framework for revitalising distressed assets.
- Where the courseware and the 2025 Directions differ the Directions are taught (for example DCCO deferment of up to 3 years for infrastructure and 2 years for non-infrastructure projects, and the 0.375 and 0.5625 per cent quarterly provisions).
- The calamities chapter and definition 8A (from 1 July 2026) and the SNFA paragraphs (from 1 October 2026) are after the cut-off and not taught.
- ARC rules were read only as far as the Transfer and Distribution Directions; the ARC Directions were not retrieved.

## Drafts and originality

Tables and procedures are written afresh from the paragraphs; worked examples and numerical questions use new figures (Rs 800 crore outstanding, Rs 550 crore base, Rs 1,000 crore funded outstanding, Rs 200 crore base bid). Answer positions are spread across A to D by construction. Distractor explanations are brief.
