## Limits of the evidence

- The Rules text is a consolidation published on a lawyer's website, not an official copy. The Official Gazette, DPIIT and RBI copies could not be retrieved. The widening to individuals is confirmed by RBI's own circular; the 10 per cent, 24 per cent and divestment periods rest on the consolidation.
- The courseware's 5 and 10 per cent Portfolio Investment Scheme limits and the ten-year property lock-in are not taught; the first was superseded on 12 June 2026, the second is absent from RBI's current Master Direction.
- Sectoral caps (Schedule I) and the FIRMS reporting workflow are not covered.
- The reviewer is the authoring agent; no independent or human review.
