## Difference found between the courseware and the Directions

The courseware presents the Basic Indicator Approach, the Standardised Approach and the AMA as the Basel II menu and mentions the Reserve Bank's new approach only as a description. The Forthcoming Instructions Directions state that the new approach applies only when their effective date is communicated separately; no date was found, so the items teach the Basic Indicator Approach as the approach now in force and the new approach as announced.

## Limits of the evidence

- No effective date for the Basel III Standardised Approach was found in the copy read; a later notification was not searched for.
- The courseware's ₹1,00,000 loss threshold and 100 per cent of BIC rule for banks outside loss data standards could not be matched to the Directions text and are not tested; the items use the Directions' rule (capital equals BIC for bucket 1 or fewer than five years of data).
- The internal loss multiplier formula was not recomputed.
- The Basel Committee principles (2011), event types and betas, and the courseware's account of the April 2017 Reserve Bank advice on the Chief Risk Officer, were not checked against primary texts.
- The Guidance Note on operational risk management and the outsourcing Directions were not read.
- The courseware's own illustrations are not reproduced; all figures are new.
- The reviewer is the authoring agent; no independent or human review.
