## Agreement and differences between courseware and Directions

The courseware matches the Directions on governance, the structural liquidity statement parts, the stock ratio definitions and the overseas norms. Additions in the Directions used here: the 5, 10, 15 and 20 per cent limits on net cumulative negative mismatches in the first four buckets (the courseware pages read do not state them), the five business day and one calendar month minimum stress periods, six-monthly variance analysis, and Basel principles reviewed in January 2019. The courseware's industry averages for the stock ratios and its time lags for submitting returns are not in the Directions and are not tested.

## Limits of the evidence
